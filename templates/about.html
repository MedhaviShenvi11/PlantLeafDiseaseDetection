About AgriVision AI

Artificial Intelligence based Plant Leaf Disease Detection System

Project Overview

AgriVision AI is a machine learning web application that detects plant leaf diseases using image processing and deep learning. Farmers can upload a leaf image and receive disease predictions, confidence scores, and treatment suggestions instantly.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower farmers with Artificial Intelligence and help improve crop productivity through early disease detection.

🚀 Our Vision

To build intelligent agricultural solutions that contribute to sustainable farming and food security.
